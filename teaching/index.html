Credentials

Foundations for University Teaching in Statistics and Actuarial Science Certificate

To earn this certificate, graduate students in the University of Waterloo’s Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science are required to attend at least six workshops (two run by the department and at least four run by the university’s Centre for Teaching Excellence) and complete two teaching practicums observed by a member of the department’s TA Program.

My first practicum involved running a 50-minute tutorial on random variable transformations (STAT 330, Fall 2024). My second practicum involved designing and running a two-hour review session on finding maximum likelihood, method of moments, and Bayesian estimators and evaluating their efficiency (STAT 450, Winter 2025). Both were observed by Prof. Reza Ramezan.

More information about the certificate can be found here.
